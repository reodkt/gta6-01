# PALM COAST — FINAL BUILD

**Game:** PALM COAST (open-world crime sandbox, browser-based)

## Systems
- **City:** 54x54-tile procedurally generated grid — roads every 6 tiles, districts, buildings, parks, lots
- **Traffic:** AI cars driving road lanes with lane control and spawning
- **Theft:** car jacking with lock/ignition system
- **Wanted + Police:** star-based heat system, cop spawns, chases, busted logic
- **Missions:** 7 chained missions for Manny (collect / wreck / steal / survive) with dialogue and cash rewards
- **Combat:** shooting, health, damage, enemy ganging
- **Minimap HUD:** live minimap plus cash, wanted stars, health, mission tracker
- **Save:** localStorage save/restore of progress
- **Audio:** WebAudio-generated engine, sirens, gunshots (no assets)
- **Day/Night:** time cycle with dynamic lighting and headlights
